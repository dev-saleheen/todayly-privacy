Todayly

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Todayly is a personal daily planner for tasks, habits, water intake, mood, focus sessions and saved links. It is built local-first: the data you enter lives in a database on your own device. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Your data is stored on your device by default.
  • Todayly Pro is an optional subscription ($4.99/month after a 7-day free trial). It covers cloud sync across devices and a larger smart-assistant allowance. Core features and the on-device assistant stay free.
  • If you sign in with Google or create an email and password account, and turn on cloud sync, a copy of your synced records is stored in our managed backend.
  • Smart answers send your message and a short summary of today to our managed assistant service, which forwards them to a model provider to produce the reply. If you are signed out, offline, or out of allowance, the assistant runs on-device.
  • Feature requests and feedback are only sent when you press Send in Profile. The note is stored in our backend, emailed to the developer through Resend, and purged by routine processing once older than 12 months.
  • There are no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell your data.

1. Data stored on your device

Todayly keeps the following in a local database on your device:

  • Your display name, email address and avatar URL, if you choose to sign in
  • Tasks, notes and due dates
  • Habits and habit check-ins
  • Water intake entries and goals
  • Mood entries (score, tags, notes)
  • Focus session history
  • Saved links, their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and your notes
  • Assistant conversation history
  • App settings and reminder schedules

This data stays on your device unless you explicitly use one of the optional features below.

2. Optional account and cloud sync

Cloud sync is off by default. Turning it on requires an explicit sign-in: either continue with Google, or create an email and password account in the app. Passwords for email accounts are stored hashed by Supabase and are never readable by us.

  • What we receive: your account's email address, display name and avatar URL (if your Google profile provides one), and the app data needed to keep your devices in sync: water logs, habits and habit logs, tasks, mood check-ins, focus sessions, reminders, saved links, notes and assistant history.
  • Why: to authenticate you and store the data so it can be restored on another device.
  • Where: our managed sync service, hosted on Supabase, encrypted in transit over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Retention: the synced copy is kept until you delete your account or request deletion.
  • Cost: cloud sync and the higher smart-answer allowance are part of the optional Todayly Pro subscription, $4.99/month after a 7-day free trial. Payment is processed by Google Play; we receive subscription status and expiry, never your card details. You can cancel in Google Play -> Subscriptions.
  • Lawful basis: your consent, given by choosing to sign in; you can withdraw it by turning cloud sync off and deleting the account.

If you never sign in, none of this data is transmitted.

3. Smart answers (assistant)

  • Smart answers are included with the account you use for cloud sync, with a free daily allowance. An active Todayly Pro subscription raises the allowance (currently 100 answers a day; the free allowance is 3 a day). The current allowance is shown in Profile -> Todayly Pro.
  • When a smart answer is produced, your message, the recent messages from the same conversation (so follow-up questions make sense) and a short summary of today (water, habits, tasks, mood, focus) are sent over HTTPS to our managed assistant service (a Supabase Edge Function). That service forwards the request to a model provider - Groq as the primary provider, with Cloudflare Workers AI as a possible fallback - to produce the answer.
  • Voice input: the recorded clip is sent the same way and transcribed by the provider. Spoken replies use the device's text-to-speech engine and never leave the phone.
  • What we store: nothing from the conversation or the audio. We keep only a per-day and per-minute counter (account id, feature, window, count) so the daily allowance can be enforced; those counters are deleted with your account.
  • Provider handling: the request is used only to produce the reply. We enable the model provider's zero-data-retention (ZDR) setting, so inference inputs and outputs are not logged or retained, and under our agreement the provider does not train or fine-tune models on them. A fallback provider, if we ever enable one, is used only to produce the reply.
  • On-device fallback: if you are signed out, offline, out of allowance, or the app deliberately keeps the request out of scope, the assistant answers on-device; no audio or message leaves the device.
  • Lawful basis: your consent, given by sending a message while signed in; you can withdraw it by turning smart answers off or signing out.
  • AI responses are generated by third-party models and may be inaccurate. They are not medical, legal or financial advice.

4. Feature requests and feedback

Profile -> Feature requests & feedback lets you send a note to the developer. Sending is always your choice: the form works signed out, and nothing is sent until you press Send.

  • What we receive: the note you type, whether it is a feature request or feedback, the app version and platform, and when you sent it. Optionally, an email address you enter if you would like a reply. If you are signed in, the report is linked to your account id; if you are signed out, we store only a keyed pseudo-random hash of your IP address (a pseudonymous identifier, not anonymity), used solely to rate-limit abuse. We do not store raw IP addresses.
  • Why: to read your idea or problem, reply when you asked us to, and improve the app. Feedback is never used for advertising, profiling or model training.
  • Where: our managed Supabase backend, then emailed to the developer through Resend. Email is how the note reaches the developer, and the delivered copy stays in their mailbox.
  • Retention: we keep the stored report for no longer than 12 months. At the next successful feedback submission, reports older than 12 months are removed from our database (there is no background scheduler, so a dormant service can hold an old report slightly longer; ask us and we delete the copy we store on request). The delivered email copy stays in the developer's mailbox so the note can still be read. Deleting your account deletes the reports linked to it. Reports sent while signed out are not tied to your account; the optional contact email and the keyed IP hash remain pseudonymous identifiers, not anonymity.
  • Lawful basis: your consent, given by pressing Send. You can withdraw it by asking us to delete the copy we store.
  • Rate limits: to protect the service and its email quota, submissions are rate-limited per account and per IP address, and overly frequent reports are refused. Short-lived rate-limit counters (date, count, and the account id or keyed IP hash - never the note text) are kept for up to 40 days. Deleting an account removes any counter that carries its id; the aggregate daily counts contain no identifiers and are kept only for the 40-day window.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone - only while you record a voice command or voice search.
  • Notifications - for the reminders and focus-session alerts you schedule. These notifications are local to your device; Todayly does not use push notifications.
  • Vibration - for haptic feedback.
  • Internet - for optional sync, smart answers when you are signed in with allowance left, and a feature request or feedback note you choose to send.

The app requests no access to your location, contacts, calendar, photos, files or SMS.

7. Security

Everything transmitted uses HTTPS/TLS. Synced data is scoped to your account on the server. Device data is protected by the operating system's app sandbox. No method is perfectly secure, but we keep access to the minimum needed for sync, smart answers and feedback delivery to work.

8. Children

Todayly is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Health information

Water, mood, habit and focus entries are self-tracking tools. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Todayly is not a medical device.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Export: the app can export your data through the system share sheet.
  • Erase local data: Profile -> Erase all data removes everything from the device.
  • Delete account and synced data: see the next section.
  • Withdraw a feedback report: email us with enough detail to find it (the report id is in the note's email). We delete the copy we store; an email already delivered to the developer's mailbox remains unless you also ask us to delete it there. Reports linked to an account are also deleted with the account; reports sent while signed out are deleted on request.
  • Access / correction: you can edit or export your data at any time; for server-side copies, contact us.

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may also have the right to object to processing, request a portable copy, or lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Profile -> Erase all data removes your local database contents.
  • Account and synced copies: Profile -> Todayly Pro -> Delete account deletes your account and every synced row, including the assistant allowance counter and any feature requests or feedback linked to the account. You can also turn cloud sync off to stop syncing. If you cannot open the app, email the contact address below from the account's email address and we delete the account and synced rows within 30 days. A feedback note already delivered to the developer's mailbox is not covered by account deletion; ask us if you want that removed too.
  • Feedback sent while signed out: it is not tied to an account, so it cannot be deleted by account deletion; it is deleted on request, or purged by routine processing after 12 months.
  • Everything: uninstalling Todayly removes all local app data, including secure storage.
  • AI provider data: requests sent for a smart answer are handled under the provider's policy; we do not store the conversation.
